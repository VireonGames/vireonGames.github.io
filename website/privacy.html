Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

VireonGames is a game launcher made by VIREON Interactive. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and the username you choose. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and name from Google. We never see your Google password.
  • Your library: which games you've added to your library or wishlist, and when you download them.
  • Messages: anything you send us through the Contact page.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account, including sending sign-in codes to your email.
  • To show your games, wishlist and downloads in the launcher.
  • To understand how many people play and download our games.
  • To reply to your messages.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't share your information with anyone except the services that run VireonGames: Supabase stores our data and Google handles Google sign-in.

Games you install

Games are saved on your own computer. You can remove them at any time from your Library.

Deleting your account

Email us at vireoninteractive@gmail.com and we will delete your account and its data.

Children

VireonGames is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has made an account, email us and we will delete it.
